Gel / Western Blot Label Maker

Set up your wells, group shared samples, add per-well treatment labels, then export as an image to paste into Benchling.
Use +◀ / ▶+ on a well to insert a new blank well right before/after it. Drag a well by its ⠿ handle to reorder columns, or use the ◀ ▶ buttons to nudge it one position at a time. Once a well has a group name, a small blue square appears in the corner of its cell — drag that across adjacent wells to fill them with the same group name instead of retyping it.
Grouping: To group wells that share a base sample (e.g. the same cell line across time points), type the same text into the "Group / base sample" cell for each well in that contiguous run — or type it once and drag the blue fill handle in the corner of that cell across the adjacent wells you want to include. Wells with matching, contiguous group text will be bracketed together with the group name shown once above. Leave the group cell blank for wells that don't belong to a group.
Treatment label: Use this row for anything specific to that individual lane (timepoint, dose, +/- treatment, replicate number, etc).
Copying to Benchling: "Copy Image" places the rendered label directly on your clipboard so you can paste (Ctrl/Cmd+V) straight into a Benchling notebook entry. If your browser blocks clipboard image access, use "Download PNG" and insert the image file instead.
